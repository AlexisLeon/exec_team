# Decisions

Dated log of decisions and why. Append-only.

**Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`

**Why this exists:** six weeks from now you will not remember whether you picked
per-case pricing because of customer evidence or because of a bad morning. The log is
how you tell the difference, and how you avoid relitigating settled questions.

**Rules:**
- One decision per file.
- Record what you *expected* to happen. That's what makes it reviewable.
- Never edit the decision. To change course, write a new file that supersedes it,
  and add a `supersedes:` line.
- Reversed decisions stay. They're the most instructive ones.

Use `/decide` to write one.
