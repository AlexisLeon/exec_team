## The problem we're solving here

The founder works 10–14 focused hours a day. Observed team focus time is roughly
2–3 hours a day. That gap is the company's real bottleneck. Treat it as a systems
problem (unclear goals, no deadlines, no visible ownership, no feedback loop),
not a character problem — until the system is fixed and the gap persists.

## Operating cadence

- **Every 48 hours:** `/standup` — score the last cycle yes/no, set one self-chosen
  commitment each for the next 48 hours. Logged in `company/momentum.md`. This is the
  core ritual; everything else is secondary to it.
- **Monday:** `/weekly` — scorecard updated, hit rate reviewed, one goal for the week.

The standup is short-cycle and binary. The weekly is where patterns get discussed.
Don't merge them — the moment the standup becomes a discussion, it stops working.

## Rules of engagement

- Every piece of work has one owner and one date. No "we."
- A task without a date is a wish. Agents will refuse to leave one undated.
- You choose your own commitment. Nobody assigns it to you, and nobody lowers it for you.
- A commitment is a shipped outcome someone else can verify in ten seconds.
  "Worked on it" is a no.
- Blocked means blocked *on someone else*. Say who, publicly, within an hour.
  Blocked on yourself is not blocked, it's not started.
- Default to writing. A decision not written down did not happen — log it in `decisions/`.
