# Momentum ledger

Every commitment made, and whether it shipped. Append-only. Updated every 48 hours
by `/standup`.

This file exists because momentum is not a feeling, it's a hit rate you can see.
One glance down the Shipped column tells you more about the company than the scorecard.

**Rules**
- Yes or no. No partial credit, no "mostly," no percentages. A story is a no.
- The person sets their own commitment. Assigned work goes in `company/team.md`, not here.
- The founder is in the ledger on the same terms as everyone else.
- Never edit a past row. A wrong row stays and gets a note.
