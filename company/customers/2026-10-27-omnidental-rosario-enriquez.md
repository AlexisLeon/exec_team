## Situation

Sends us **all** her restorations, and has gone all in on 3D printed permanent resin
rather than zirconia or lithium disilicate. No quality issues reported to date.

Came to us from **Futura**, a previous lab.

## Why she buys (founder's read — unverified with her)

Probably not the material's clinical benefits. More likely:

- **Price.** Roughly **half** the cost of zirconia or lithium disilicate.
- **Margin.** A cheaper permanent restoration widens her margin per case.
- **Speed.** 3D printing has none of the production-time limits of milled ceramics.

> **This is an assumption, not a finding.** Ask her directly which of the three
> actually drives it. The answer determines whether we are a price play or a
> speed play, and we are currently guessing.
