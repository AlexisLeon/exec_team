As the `cofounder` agent, tell Alex what to do today. $ARGUMENTS

Read `company/strategy.md` (the one goal and the gap), `company/scorecard.md` (what's
moving), `company/team.md` (what's committed), and anything new in
`company/customers/`.

Then, short — this should be readable in thirty seconds:

```
**Goal:** <the one number, and the gap, from strategy.md>
**Today, in order:**
1. <the 90-minute prospecting block, unless there's a real reason it moves>
2. <the one thing that moves the goal>
3. <the one thing that unblocks someone else on the team>
**Don't:** <the appealing thing he'll be tempted into — usually engineering>
**Check in on:** <who owes what today>
```

If something in the files is stale or empty enough that you'd be guessing, say so
instead of guessing.
