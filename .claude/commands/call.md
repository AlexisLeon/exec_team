---

File a customer conversation into `company/customers/`.

Take whatever the founder gives you — a transcript, rough notes, a voice memo
transcription, or just a verbal recap — and turn it into a proper record.

1. If he hasn't given you the raw material yet, ask for it. Then ask only for what's
   genuinely missing: company, person, title, how they came to us, what was agreed.
2. Write `company/customers/YYYY-MM-DD-company-slug.md` using `_template.md`.
   **Preserve their exact words in the "In their words" section.** Do not clean up
   their phrasing, do not paraphrase into marketing language. Verbatim is the asset.
3. Be honest in the "Trigger" and "Next step" sections. If no next step was agreed on
   the call, write that — it's the most useful finding in the file.
4. Then, as the `gtm` agent, give the read: is this real pipeline (can sign, trigger,
   date), what's the next move with a date, and what you'd have asked that he didn't.
5. Flag anything that contradicts `company/strategy.md` — ICP, pain, pricing,
   positioning. If it does, say so clearly and offer to log a decision.
6. Add any new objection to the running tally for the `cmo` agent.
