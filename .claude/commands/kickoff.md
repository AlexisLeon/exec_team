description: One-time setup interview — fills strategy.md, voice.md, team.md and the scorecard
---

Run the founding interview. Use the `cofounder` agent's voice and standards.

This is an interview, not a form. Ask questions **one at a time**, in small batches of
at most three, and react to the answers. Push back when an answer is vague — "dental
clinics" is not an ICP, "grow revenue" is not a goal. Keep pushing on a question until
the answer is specific enough to act on, then move on.

Cover, in this order:

1. **What we do** — one sentence a stranger understands.
2. **The one goal** — one number, one date. Refuse three goals. Get today's number too.
3. **ICP** — push until he could name 50 real companies. Get the buyer title, the
   champion, the trigger event, and who we disqualify.
4. **The pain** — what breaks, what it costs, what they do today instead of us.
5. **Positioning** — category, the one thing only we can say.
6. **Pricing** — model, number, why, what's been tested, the objection.
7. **Distribution** — what's worked inbound, what outbound will be.
8. **The bet** — the one assumption that, if wrong, kills the plan.
9. **Team** — names, what outcome each owns, what the real output gap looks like.
10. **Voice** — ask him to paste two things he's written that he'd be happy to see again.

Then:
- Write `company/strategy.md`, `company/team.md`, and the voice samples into
  `company/voice.md`. Fill `company/scorecard.md` with today's baseline row and the
  metric definitions he agreed to.
- Log `company/decisions/<today>-founding-strategy.md` capturing the goal, the ICP,
  and the bet, with what we expect to happen.
- Finish with the cofounder's read: the single biggest risk, and the one thing to do
  tomorrow morning.

Do not invent answers. If he doesn't know something, write `?` and note how we'd find out.
