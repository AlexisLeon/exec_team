description: Monday operating review — scorecard, pipeline, decisions, one goal for the week
---

Run the weekly review. You are chairing it as the `cofounder` agent, and you pull in
the `gtm` and `cmo` agents for their sections. Keep the whole thing tight.

**1. Numbers.** Read `company/scorecard.md`. Ask for this week's numbers — one
question with all the metrics, not one at a time. Append the new row. Any metric the
founder can't answer gets `?`, and a `?` that appears twice in a row becomes an action
item to instrument it.

**2. Momentum.** Read `company/momentum.md`. Compute the week's commitment hit rate
per person and overall, and write it into the scorecard row. This is a pattern
conversation, not a scoring one — the standups already did the scoring. Look across
cycles: who is trending up, who is sandbagging (always committing to exactly what they
can comfortably finish), whose commitments keep getting redefined mid-cycle. Name what
slipped and whether it's the system or the person.

If fewer than three standups happened this week, stop and deal with that before
anything else on this list. The ritual lapsing is a more urgent problem than any
number on the scorecard.

**3. GTM section.** Dispatch to the `gtm` agent: pipeline review, which deals are dead,
did the daily prospecting block happen, what's the top objection this week.

**4. CMO section.** Dispatch to the `cmo` agent: what did we learn about the message,
what's the one change to make, is the site saying what outbound is saying.

**5. Decisions due for review.** Check `company/decisions/` for any with a `review_on`
date that has passed. For each, fill in what actually happened versus what we expected.

**6. One goal for the week.** Exactly one, with a number and a date, for the founder
and for each person in `company/team.md`. Write them into the scorecard's notes section.

**7. The cofounder's close.** Two or three sentences: what's actually working, what
you're worried about, and the one thing that must happen before Friday.

Update `company/scorecard.md` and `company/team.md`. Log any real decision made during
the review to `company/decisions/`.
